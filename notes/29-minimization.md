# 29 Minimization

Note 05 ended with the promise that momentum and Adam exist because of what the plain gradient descent loop cannot do. `calccode/optimize.py` now has the payoff: golden section search for 1D brackets, an Armijo backtracking line search, Newton's method for minimization, BFGS, and the three gradient descent variants, all on the hand-written gradient and Hessian from `multivar.py`.

The line search is the piece that makes everything else safe. It starts at a full step and halves alpha until f drops by at least a fraction c of what the linear model promised. Newton on a quadratic bowl needs exactly one solve of Hessian d = -g (two iterations counting the tolerance check), but on Rosenbrock the raw step can point uphill, so the code falls back to steepest descent whenever g . d is not negative. BFGS avoids the Hessian entirely. It starts with the identity and folds curvature in from the iterate pairs, and on Rosenbrock from (-1.2, 1) it reaches (1, 1) in 35 iterations.

The variant comparison is the satisfying one. Plain gradient descent with lr = 0.001 needs 9339 steps to push Rosenbrock below 1e-4. Momentum does it in 836, Nesterov in 874, Adam in 873. Momentum's path still oscillates; 17 of its first 300 steps increase the loss. Adam with a large lr = 0.5 is worse: its first step sends the loss from 24.2 to 104.9, and a later one jumps 4.2 to 46.4. Per-coordinate step sizes buy speed, not stability.

Where this breaks: BFGS on a non-convex function with several basins. On Himmelblau's function, which has four equal minima, the start point picks the winner. From (0, 0) it lands at (3, 2); from (4, 4) at (-3.78, -3.28); from (0, 4) at (-2.81, 3.13). Every run reports converged = True, because the gradient vanishes at all four. Worse, the positive-definite story quietly fails on the way down: from (4, 4), one of the ten curvature pairs has y . s <= 0, so that update gets skipped to keep H sane. BFGS is a local method wearing a global method's confidence. The only honest fixes are restarts or a better start, and neither fits inside the loop.
